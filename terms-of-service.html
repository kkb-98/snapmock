SnapMock is a Chrome Extension that captures screenshots and wraps them in device mockup frames. The Extension provides both free and paid (Pro) features.

The free version includes basic capture, device frames, backgrounds, and annotation tools (Arrow, Text, Box). Pro features are unlocked with a one-time purchase of a license key through Lemon Squeezy.
